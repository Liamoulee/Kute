Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Short version. Kute is free and open source. It needs no account with us, shows no ads and sells nothing. What the client sends to our server is only what it needs to work. The client sends no telemetry.

1. This website

kute.lol is served by our own server behind Cloudflare. To deliver the pages, Cloudflare and our server process your IP address and the usual request data (URL, browser, time). Our server's log records the requested path, not your IP address, and is deleted after 31 days.

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It loads the current player count from our own server.

2. The Kute client

Kute badge and player count

While the client runs, it keeps a connection to our server. When you are logged in to Krunker, it sends a hash of the lobby id and your in-game display name, so that other Kute users in the same match can see the Kute badge next to you. The hash is only held in memory while you are connected and is never stored. The server also counts open connections and how many clients were started for the first time. The client remembers itself that it was counted, the server keeps no list.

If you do not want any contact with our server, add *://kute.lol/* to user_blocklist.json in Documents\kute. The client plays the same without it.

Rate limiting

To protect the server from abuse, request counts are kept per IP address. The address is replaced by a salted hash in memory. The salt is never stored and changes on every restart. Entries are deleted within 75 minutes.

Other services

  • Updates: the client checks GitHub (api.github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com) for new versions. GitHub's privacy statement applies to these requests.
  • Krunker: the game itself is run by its operator. Their privacy policy applies. Kute does not change what Krunker collects.
  • Discord: with Rich Presence on, the client tells the Discord app on your PC your current mode and map. Discord's privacy policy applies.

Stored only on your PC

Settings, userscripts, swapped files, the browser cache, crash logs and saved accounts stay in Documents\kute. Saved account passwords are encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) and are never sent to us.

4. Changes

We update this policy when the client or the website changes what they process. The date at the top shows the current version.
