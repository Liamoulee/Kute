Prices and evidence

The Kute estimate uses recent completed daily sales aggregates, with older history when trading is sparse. It accounts for unusual averages and lower competing asks. When there are no recent sales, the estimate falls back to asking prices, Krunker's estimate, or a game quick-sell value. The item page tells you which evidence is available.

Recent sales and sparse sales describe the supporting data. A heuristic evidence score is not a probability that a price is correct. Historical price ranges describe daily averages, not every transaction or a prediction interval.

Reading the charts

Both modes include line and candle charts, comparisons, zoom, pan, and daily sales bars. Line charts show daily average sale prices, except the 1D range which uses observed asks. Ask candles show the open, high, low, and close of sampled quotes, including historical quote changes. Gaps are retained. Volume bars show official daily sale counts where available.

Advanced analytics

Advanced adds daily-price moving averages, RSI, volatility, drawdown, sales-weighted prices, activity comparisons, and an ask-side depth ladder. These describe completed observations. Moving averages and RSI need consecutive priced days; missing data stays unavailable. Market diagnostics show breadth, return dispersion, category performance, and turnover concentration. Metric explanations and coverage appear alongside the numbers.

Activity and selling

Activity grades combine historical sales frequency, listed supply, and the relationship between asks and estimated value. They do not promise a selling time. The letter badges on market items use this scale; they measure activity support, separately from price confidence.

A
Very active

Strongest activity score. Frequent sales and supportive asking prices relative to the estimate.

B
Active

A strong activity score, with less support than grade A. Check recent sales and competing listings.

C
Moderate

Mixed trading activity and pricing support. Selling may require more patience or a more competitive ask.

D
Thin

Weak activity support. Sparse sales or less supportive asking prices make an exit harder to assess.

F
Very limited

The lowest activity score, including items without a sales-based estimate. Check the available evidence carefully.

The asking-price planner compares your proposed price with observed competitors; its gross proceeds assume all units sell and exclude listing fees.

Trade comparisons

A verdict compares the estimated value of the two sides and becomes uncertain when an item cannot be reliably valued. Advanced liquidity adjustments are heuristic discounts, not guaranteed liquidation prices. Rare instance premiums remain unknown until supported by validated sales.

Your watchlist and inventory

Watchlists, target prices, and your preferred view are stored on this device. Target checks run in the menu and pause during gameplay. Inventory history applies historical prices to today's holdings. It does not measure personal profit.

Your data

With Kute Market enabled, Kute reads your recent completed market sales and private trade history in the menu and contributes the transaction facts: item IDs, quantities, available serial/wear/paint attributes, currencies, reported dates and transaction IDs. Names, account IDs, trade messages and login tokens are removed before submission. An opaque session hash groups reports for validation. Private trades supply evidence the public market APIs do not provide. Reported exchange comparisons are separate from market estimates; duplicate and conflicting reports are checked before use. Opening or closing a draft trade does not report it as completed. Disable Kute Market to stop collection and market requests, or disable online features to stop all contact with Kute's server.

You make the trade

Kute does not buy, list, or trade items for you. Check the source, freshness, and trading activity alongside the estimate. Predictive prices and sale probabilities will only appear when their accuracy has been evaluated.
